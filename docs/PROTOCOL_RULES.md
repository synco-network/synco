# SYNCO Protocol Rules

## 1. Identity
- Blockchain: SYNCO
- Native gas coin: SYNCO
- Symbol: SYNCO
- Decimals: 18

## 2. Supply
- Maximum supply: 10,000,000 SYNCO
- New minting: Disabled
- Inflation: Disabled
- Supply must never exceed the maximum supply.

## 3. Initial Allocation
- Staking and Validator: 1,000,000 SYNCO (10%)
- Users and Ecosystem: 8,500,000 SYNCO (85%)
- Developer: 500,000 SYNCO (5%)

## 4. Gas Fee
- Target fee for a simple SYNCO transfer: USD 0.0001
- Fee payment currency: SYNCO
- Pricing reference: USDC
- Dynamic pricing based on network congestion
- Smart contract transactions pay according to actual gas usage.
- The target fee is not a guarantee or a hard cap.

## 5. Staking and Validators
- Staking rewards must not come from new minting.
- Validator rewards are funded from collected transaction fees.
- Staking and validator participation must follow protocol rules.

## 6. Monetary Policy
- No additional token issuance.
- No automatic inflation.
- Bridge accounting must prevent duplicate supply.
- Total circulating and locked supply must remain within the 10,000,000 SYNCO maximum.

## 7. Development Status
- Current stage: Local development
- Network: Not deployed
- Testnet: Not deployed
- Mainnet: Not deployed
